About

Smitha G S is a self-taught artist from the Malabar region of Kozhikode, Kerala. She did not come to painting through school competitions, where being handed a theme never suited her. Her work grew out of memory.

Her canvases are dense, saturated forests with chameleons, grasshoppers, micro-organisms and aquatic creatures moving through them. She paints in green as a memorial to her father, who made toys from coconut leaves for her when she was a child.

She works in acrylic. Her paintings were first shown in "Lokame Tharavadu", curated by Bose Krishnamachari, and later at the Kochi-Muziris Biennale 2022–23, where one of them travelled further than most: onto the tail of an aircraft.

Spending time in the forest allowed me to intentionally examine the life and creatures that sometimes appear inconsequential.Smitha G S, in The Week

Journey

  1. 2017

    Lalitha Kala Akademi Award for one of her paintings.

  2. 2022–23

    Kochi-Muziris Biennale, fifth edition. Her acrylic painting was shown at Aspinwall House, Fort Kochi.

  3. 26 January 2023

    Tail art, Air India Express. A 25-foot adaptation of her painting was unveiled on Boeing 737-800 VT-AXN.

  4. March 2023

    Felicitated by Air India Express on Women's Day, with CIAL and Kochi Biennale Foundation.

  5. 2024

    Forest Festival of the Arts, Okayama, Japan. She showed Eudaimonia.
