About the artist

A self-taught artist whose work grows out of memory, and whose forests are never empty.

Smitha G S is a self-taught artist from the Malabar region of Kozhikode, Kerala. Her canvases are dense, saturated forests with chameleons, grasshoppers, micro-organisms and aquatic creatures moving through them.

She paints in green as a memorial to her father, who made toys from coconut leaves for her when she was a child.

Her paintings were first shown in “Lokame Tharavadu”, curated by Bose Krishnamachari, and later at the Kochi-Muziris Biennale 2022–23, where one of them travelled further than most: onto the tail of an aircraft.
